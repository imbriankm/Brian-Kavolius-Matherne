# Perfect competition — Stage 3 (Analysis + Prompt Log)

Professor Stauffer's assignment page is the source: [Stage 3 — Analysis + Prompt Log](https://adamwstauffer.github.io/ai-lms/case-perfect-competition-stage3.html). This is a checklist of what it asks for, not a copy of the page. The page gives no due date.

## The four questions the analysis answers, from my workbook

1. Where the highest-revenue crop stops, and why there.
2. Which constraints bind and which are slack, and what one more unit of each binding one is worth.
3. Whether any marginal cost curve falls, and the mechanism behind it.
4. Whether each crop makes money alone, carrying the whole fixed cost, and whether that matters (MC against AVC).

## Deliverables

- [ ] Analysis, one to two pages: `analysis/YYYY-MM-DD-perfect-competition-analysis.md`
- [ ] At least two figures from my workbook in `analysis/figures/`, each shown inline where the text refers to it
- [ ] Figures render on github.com (relative paths, exact capitals)
- [ ] Analysis cites cells, covers all four questions, and ends with an honest paragraph against my Stage 1 hypothesis (14 / 20 / 30)
- [ ] Memo, half a page: `docs/decisions/YYYY-MM-DD-perfect-competition-memo.md` (the plan, the judgment call, what would change my answer)
- [ ] Prompt log: a dated section for this engagement in `prompt-log.md`, curated sessions
- [ ] Reflection of 300 words or fewer, naming something concrete I verified and how
- [ ] `capabilities/marginal-analysis/README.md`: update the "exercised in:" line
- [ ] At least two descriptive commits for this stage

## Where AI may and may not help

- I write the analysis, the memo and the reflection myself, and commit them first.
- After that commit, AI may edit for structure only. I apply what I agree with in my own words and commit again.
- The reflection is never AI-touched.
- AI may keep the bookkeeping half of the prompt log (date, tool, what I asked, what it produced). It never writes the reflection, what it got wrong, or how I verified.
